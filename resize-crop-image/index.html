Resize and Crop FAQ

Does resizing upload my image?

The quick resize and crop tool runs locally in your browser, so the image stays on your device.

What size should I use for Pinterest?

A vertical 2:3 crop is a common starting point for Pinterest pins.

Can I crop for Instagram?

Yes. Square and portrait crops are useful for many Instagram posts and previews.
