Image Compressor FAQ

Is the image uploaded to a server?

The quick compressor runs in your browser, so the image is processed locally on your device.

Which format should I choose?

JPG is good for photos, PNG is useful when transparency matters, and WebP often gives smaller files for the web.

Can compression reduce image quality?

Yes. Lower file sizes can remove detail, so choose the highest quality that still gives you a useful file size.
