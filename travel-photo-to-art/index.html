Travel Photo to Art FAQ

Can I turn architecture photos into art?

Yes. Buildings, monuments, streets, and scenic views usually work well because their shapes are clear.

What prompt should I use?

Try concise prompts like watercolor postcard, hand-painted travel poster, or soft ink illustration.

Can I use the result on Pinterest?

Yes, if you have rights to the original photo. Before-and-after travel pins are a strong way to show the transformation.
