Photo to Comic FAQ

Can I use this for a group photo?

Yes. Clear group photos can work, but close portraits usually keep facial details more consistently.

What kind of photo works best?

Use sharp images with the subject near the center. Avoid extreme blur, very dark lighting, or tiny faces.

Is it free to try?

PictTool includes free daily usage with limited AI generations, so you can test the effect before deciding what to create next.
