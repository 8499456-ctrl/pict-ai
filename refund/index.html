Refund Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

This Refund Policy applies to Pict Pro subscriptions purchased through Paddle.

1. Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time. Cancellation stops the next renewal; you can continue using the service until the end of the current billing period unless otherwise stated.

2. Refunds

If you are not satisfied with your first Pict Pro purchase, you may request a refund within 7 days of the initial charge. Refund requests for renewal charges are reviewed case by case. Unless required by law, partial-period refunds are not provided after a renewal has started.

3. How to request a refund

Use the Feedback form on PictTool and include the account email used for the purchase, the charge date, and a brief reason. Paddle may process the refund and may ask for transaction details to locate the payment.

4. Processing time

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method. Processing time depends on Paddle and your payment provider.
