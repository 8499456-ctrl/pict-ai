We use information to provide and secure the service, process subscriptions, respond to requests, improve performance, prevent abuse, and send updates when you subscribe to them.

5. Sharing and retention

We share information with service providers that help us host, authenticate, analyze, process, and bill the service. We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for these purposes, legal obligations, and dispute resolution.

You may request access to or deletion of account information by using the Feedback form on PictTool. You can cancel marketing emails through the unsubscribe option in those emails.
